How AgriConnect Operates

A seamless 4-step digital journey from tender discovery to bank account settlement.

1

Demand Posting

Corporate agribusinesses publish required grain varieties, volumes, moisture specs, and fixed offered prices.

2

Farmer Application

Cultivators inspect specifications, calculate projected returns, and submit commitments for partial or full tonnage.

3

Digital Review

Buyers evaluate land size, soil survey records, and approve bids to execute enforceable bilateral agreements.

4

Harvest & Payout

Produce delivered to certified logistical checkpoints triggers instant electronic direct bank transfer to farmers.
